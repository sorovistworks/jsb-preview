What construction materials does JSB Infraproducts supply?

Aggregates, ready mix concrete, OPC and PPC cement, fly ash, concrete admixtures, micro silica, AAC blocks and slab ice. One supplier covers the full material list for a typical concrete project.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Raigad and the wider Mumbai Metropolitan Region. Main plants are in Goregaon, Mira Road and Mankoli, with tie-up plants in Bhayander, Kandivali, Powai, Thane and Wadala.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Our team responds within 24 hours. Include the material, grade, quantity, site location and target dates for the fastest quote. For urgent needs, call or WhatsApp +91 93269 89258.

Who does JSB supply to?

Real estate developers, civil contractors, infrastructure projects and RMC plants. Clients include Hubtown, Hiranandani, DGS Group, AH Construction, Unwal, H. Rishabraj and AMI, along with other infrastructure contractors. Channel partners and tie-ups include UltraTech, JSW, Prism RMC, Procon RMC and RDC.

Is material tested before dispatch?

Yes. Every batch is lab-tested against the relevant Indian Standard before it leaves the plant.

Can you deliver during the monsoon or at night?

Yes. Our owned fleet runs around the clock with slot-based scheduling, so deliveries can be aligned with night pours and monsoon windows.
